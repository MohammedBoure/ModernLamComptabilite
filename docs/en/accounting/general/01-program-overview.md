# 01 - Functional Overview

## Purpose

ModernLam Accounting Software is an internal system designed to organize accounting and management for the ModernLam laboratory. The software turns current manual tables into structured screens, workflows, and reports.

## Target Audience

| Audience | Main Need |
| --- | --- |
| Accounting | Enter cash, safe, suppliers, payments, and monthly balance data. |
| Direction | Monitor revenue, expenses, profitability, and remaining balances. |
| Human resources | Track employees, contracts, attendance, leave, and salaries. |
| Cash desk | Enter cash closing and daily differences. |

## Main Modules

| Module | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Monthly summary and main alerts. |
| Cash Closing | Cash expenses and differences between real amount and SOFTLAM. |
| Cash & Safe | Cash movements, safe movements, entries, and exits. |
| Suppliers | Invoices, payments, and remaining balances. |
| Subcontractors & Conventions | External partners and conventions. |
| Attendance & Salaries | Attendance, absences, guards, and salaries. |
| Reports | Reports, printing, and export. |
| HR | Employees, contracts, and leave. |

## Monthly Operating Principle

All operations are attached to a month and a year. The month is opened, operations are entered, results are verified, then the month is closed after validation.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Data can be entered. |
| Under review | Data is being checked before closing. |
| Closed | The month is closed and can only be modified with authorization. |

## Expected Results

- Monthly cash total.
- Safe total and real safe net.
- Differences between Real Amount and Virtual Amount.
- Supplier, subcontractor, and convention remaining balances.
- Monthly balance.
- Profitability and profitability after investments.
- Attendance and salary reports.
- Printable Encashment Statement.
- Cheque Statement and Service Vehicle Tracking.
