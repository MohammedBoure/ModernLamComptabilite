# 03 - Reports and Printing

## Main Reports

| Report | Period | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash Expenses | Monthly | Verify cash expenses. |
| Differences | Monthly | Track cash differences. |
| Cash Movement | Monthly | Daily cash tracking. |
| Safe Movement | Monthly | Safe tracking. |
| Monthly Balance | Monthly | Monthly result. |
| Supplier Journal | Monthly | Supplier expenses. |
| Supplier Statement | Monthly or yearly | Supplier paid and remaining amounts. |
| Subcontractor Statement | Monthly or yearly | Partner and convention tracking. |
| Attendance | Monthly | Employee attendance. |
| Salary Report | Monthly | Monthly salaries. |
| Service Vehicle | Monthly | Service vehicle expenses. |
| Cheque Statement | Yearly | Cheques and SGA account. |
| Encashment Statement | Monthly | Fiscal encashment report. |
| Employees | As needed | Employee list. |
| Contracts | As needed | Contract status. |
| Leave | Yearly | Leave balance. |

## Official Report Content

- ModernLam logo and name when required.
- Report title.
- Period.
- Print date.
- Main table.
- Totals.
- Stamp/signature area for relevant reports.

## Encashment Statement

This report is intended for fiscal declaration.

Content:

- ModernLam information.
- Month and year.
- Days of the month.
- Designation.
- Observations.
- Amounts.
- Total.
- Stamp and signature.
