# 01 - Functional Screens

## Dashboard

The screen displays the monthly summary:

- Cash CV.
- Cash C.
- TPE.
- Expenses.
- Real Safe Net.
- Global Revenue.
- Profitability.
- Alerts on differences and remaining balances.

## Cash Closing

The screen contains:

- Cash Expenses.
- Differences between Real Amount and Virtual Amount.
- Difference Statement by user.

Purpose: control daily cash and justify gaps.

## Cash & Safe

The screen contains:

- Daily Cash Movement.
- Additional Entries.
- Safe Exits.
- Profitability Movement.
- Safe Summary.

Purpose: group monthly cash and safe movements.

## Suppliers

The screen contains:

- Supplier list.
- Invoices or orders.
- Payments.
- Remaining balances.
- Expense categories.

Purpose: know paid and remaining amounts by supplier.

## Subcontractors & Conventions

The screen contains:

- Subcontractors and conventions list.
- Amounts due.
- Payments.
- Payment mode.
- Remaining balance.

Purpose: track external partners and conventions.

## Attendance

The screen contains a monthly table:

- Employee.
- Days of month.
- Attendance, absence, and guard codes.
- Remarks.

Purpose: prepare salary data.

## Salary Report

The screen contains:

- Employees.
- Base salaries.
- Extra attendance.
- Guards.
- Absences.
- Bonuses.
- Penalties.
- Advances.
- Final salary.

Purpose: verify and validate monthly salaries.

## Reports

The module groups:

- Supplier Statement.
- Subcontractor Statement.
- Service Vehicle Tracking.
- Cheque Statement.
- Encashment Statement.

Purpose: verification, printing, and export.

## HR

The module contains:

- Employees.
- Contracts.
- Leave.
- Employee attendance.
- Employee salaries.
- Documents when needed.

Purpose: keep the employee administrative file.
