# 05 - Report and Statement Interfaces

## Principles

Each statement must allow:

- Period selection.
- Search and filters.
- Printing.
- PDF export.
- Excel export.
- Generation date and user.

## 05.1 Supplier Statement

Sections:

- Total Suppliers.
- Total Equipment.
- Total Consumables.
- Detailed suppliers.

Columns:

- Supplier.
- Amount.
- Payment.
- Remaining Balance.
- Observation.

## 05.2 Subcontractor Statement

Sections:

- All.
- Subcontractors.
- Conventions.
- Partner details.

Columns:

- ID.
- Name.
- Amount.
- Payment.
- Remaining Balance.
- Date.
- Payment.
- Observation.

## 05.3 Service Vehicle Tracking

Columns:

- Date.
- Amount.
- Details.
- Mileage.
- GPL / Extra Kilometre.
- Essence / Extra Kilometre.

Calculations:

- Total amount.
- Total GPL.
- Total Essence.
- Average KM/Fill.

## 05.4 SGA Account / Cheque Statement

Top fields:

- Account Amount on 31/12/previous year.
- Account Amount on current date.
- Year.

Columns:

- Number.
- Date.
- Beneficiary.
- Cheque Number.
- Amount.
- Entries.
- Exits.
- Designation.
- Month.

## 05.5 Encashment Statement

Header:

- ModernLam logo.
- Laboratory name.
- NIF.
- RIP.
- Print date.
- Statement month.

Columns:

- Number.
- Date.
- Designation.
- Observations.
- Amounts.

Report footer:

- Total.
- Stamp and signature.

## Exports

- Official PDF.
- Excel.
- Optional CSV.
