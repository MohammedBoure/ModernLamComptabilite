# 01 - Product Overview

## Purpose

ModernLam Accounting Software is an internal application for organizing accounting, statements, salaries, and HR data for the laboratory. It replaces separate spreadsheets with a coherent system.

## Users

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator | Users, permissions, periods, audit. |
| Direction | Balance, profitability, and alert consultation. |
| Accountant | Cash, safe, suppliers, payments, reports. |
| Cash Desk | Cash closing and differences. |
| HR | Employees, contracts, attendance, leave, salaries. |
| Viewer | Read-only access according to authorization. |

## Initial Scope

Included:

- Monthly period management.
- Cash and safe.
- Suppliers, subcontractors, conventions.
- Attendance, salaries, and HR.
- Reports, PDF, and Excel.
- Audit log for important modifications.

Excluded initially:

- Direct bank connection.
- Automatic SOFTLAM integration without a defined format.
- Full fiscal accounting beyond Encashment Statement.
- Detailed laboratory stock management.

## Modules

| Module | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Summary and alerts. |
| Accounting | Cash, safe, expenses, profitability. |
| Suppliers | Invoices, payments, remaining balances. |
| Subcontractors & Conventions | Partners and conventions. |
| Attendance & Salaries | Attendance, guards, absences, salaries. |
| Reports | Reports, printing, export. |
| HR | Employees, contracts, leave. |
| Administration | Permissions, settings, audit. |

## Principle

The system operates by month. Operations are attached to a period. A closed month becomes read-only, except for exceptional modification with audit trace.
